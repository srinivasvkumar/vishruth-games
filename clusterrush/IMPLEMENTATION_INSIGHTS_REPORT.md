This means wall_jump_input_hang could still be positive right after a wall jump, AND if the player is still on the wall, can_jump() returns true for jump_count < 1. But jump_count = 1 after wall jump, so this is OK — but the logic is confusing and fragile.\n\nb) Double jump timing:\n   - can_jump() allows jump if on ground, in coyote time, or wall coyote. TRUCK PHYSICS ISSUES\n─────────────────────────────────────────────────\na) Truck collision shape:\n   - BoxShape3D size = Vector3(6, 3, 3), centered at (0, 1.5, 0).\n   - The truck's position is at y=0 (on the ground). So collision extents are y=0 to y=3. The truck's top surface is at y=3.\n   - Player capsule has radius=0.3, height=1.0. When standing on ground, bottom of player is at y=0, top at y=1.0. On truck top at y=3, player bottom should be at y=3, top at y=4.0. The collision shape height (3.0) gives the player plenty of room, but if the player jumps too high, the collision extends to y=4.5.\n\nb) Truck swerve:\n   - swerve_offset is clamped to [-3.0, 3.0]. Truck width is 6.0 (extents), so from z=-3 to z=3. The swerve clamping means trucks stay exactly on the edge of the platform. If swerve_target randomly hits -1.5 or 1.5 and lerp reaches it, the truck reaches z=±7.5, which is far outside the platform width of 20 (z=-10 to z=10).\n   - Actually: swerve_target = randf_range(-1.5, 1.5), then velocity.z = swerve_offset * 5.0, and swerve_offset = lerp(swerve_offset, swerve_target, SWERVE_RATE * delta). The swerve_offset is clamped to [-3, 3] AFTER lerp. So maximum z velocity = 3 * 5 = 15. Over a physics frame, that could move the truck significantly.\n\nc) Truck physics:\n   - Truck sets velocity.x = current_speed every physics frame, THEN calls move_and_slide().\n   - If the truck is on the ground, is_on_floor() is true, so gravity doesn't apply. But if the truck falls through a gap (there are NO gaps in the ground generation — the ground is one continuous 300-unit box), this is fine. But the design intent seems to be that trucks should fall off the ground and the player jumps between them.\n   - The ground is 300 units long and continuous — there are no actual gaps in the ground. The trucks just drive along it. This means the \"gameplay\" of jumping between trucks only works if the player actively jumps on top of trucks. The truck's collision shape extends to y=3, so the player needs to jump ~2.25 units to land on top.\n\n7.
