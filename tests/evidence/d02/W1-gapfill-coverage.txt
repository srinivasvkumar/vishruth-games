LATENT DEFECT FLAGGED (documented, NOT fixed — src/** is FROZEN)
================================================================================

Input.ts:53 (gamepadconnected listener):
  this.gamepadState = navigator.getGamepads()[e.gamepad.index] ?? null;

The expression `e.gamepad.index` has no null-guard on `e.gamepad`. In
production, the browser dispatches a GamepadEvent with a `.gamepad`
property, so the listener works. However, if a test (or any non-standard
source) dispatches a bare `Event('gamepadconnected')` without a `.gamepad`
payload, the listener throws a TypeError synchronously out of
`window.dispatchEvent()`. This is documented by the test
"gamepadconnected listener throws TypeError when the event lacks a
gamepad payload (current behavior)" in input-system.test.ts. Flagged to
the orchestrator as a candidate hardening for Week 1 (add a guard:
`const gp = e.gamepad?.index != null ? navigator.getGamepads()[e.gamepad.index] : null;`).
No production change made in this task.
